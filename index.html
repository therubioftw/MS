Share Brookfield Yards with Smart Cabinetry?

Smart priced this job three months ago. If you tell them it's still alive, they offer something back.

Hold my price

You get

Smart's quoted price stays the same until you order, up to June 30.

You do

Confirm every four weeks that the job is still alive. If you don't, the hold ends.

Get a firm delivery date

You get

A firm delivery week instead of “subject to capacity”.

You do

Share the job now. Smart sees the details only once it commits to a date.

Sharing shows Smart

  • That this job is still going ahead
  • When you expect to order
  • Updates, like shop drawings starting

It never shows them

  • Who else you are pricing against them
  • Your margin, or what you quote the builder
  • Any project you haven't sent them

Materialspace never asks you to share on a manufacturer's behalf. You choose per project, and you can stop sharing at any time.
